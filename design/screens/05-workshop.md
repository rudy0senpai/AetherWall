+------------------------------------------+

## Purpose

- Discovery surface for online content and creator tools.
- Appears distinct from Library (Library = installed/local/available-to-run).
- Includes provider selector, search, import, and recommended/recent content.

## States

- Loading providers, no providers enabled, offline, no search results,
  import flow open.
