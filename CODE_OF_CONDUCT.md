# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

AetherWall is a community-driven wallpaper platform. This Code of Conduct establishes
the behaviour expectations for everyone who contributes to the project, whether
online, in design reviews, or at in-person events.

## 2. Core standards

- **Respect:** Treat all contributors with respect. Harassment, discrimination,
  and personal attacks are not tolerated.
- **Professionalism:** Keep discussions technical, evidence-based, and focused on
  the project's goals and documentation.
- **Constructive feedback:** Criticise ideas, not people. Offer alternatives and
  references when proposing changes.
- **Inclusive language:** Avoid language that excludes or alienates contributors
  of any background, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or culture.

## 3. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project repositories, issue trackers, and pull requests.
- All design and architecture review sessions.
- Any official AetherWall communication channel.
- Contributors acting on behalf of the project.

## 4. Reporting

Report violations to the project maintainers via the repository's issue tracker
(with the appropriate label) or through the private contact channel listed in the
repository's SECURITY.md. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially.

## 5. Enforcement

Violations may result in warnings, temporary contribution restrictions, or
permanent removal from the project, depending on severity. The maintainers will
document the outcome of any formal report.
